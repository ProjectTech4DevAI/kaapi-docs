# Getting Started

As a Kaapi partner, your organisation will be onboarded by the Kaapi team or one of our delivery partners. The steps below help you get into the platform once that's set up.

---

## As a first-time user

There are two ways to access Kaapi:

- **Flow A — Sign in with your registered account** (Google or email magic link). Full access to your organisation's workspace.
- **Flow B — Connect with an X-API key.** Lightweight access for running evaluations without a full user account.

Choose the flow that matches what your Kaapi administrator shared with you.

---

## Flow A — Sign in with Google or Email

### Step 1: Receive your invitation

- Your Kaapi administrator (or a delivery partner) invites your email address.
- You'll receive an invitation email from Kaapi.

### Step 2: Activate your account

- Open the invitation email and click the invite link.
- This opens `/invite`, verifies the token, and activates your account.

:::caution[Invitation link issues]

If you see **"Invitation link is invalid or has expired"**, contact to kaapi support for a new link.

:::

### Step 3: Sign in

On the login screen, choose one of:

#### Option 1 — Continue with Google

- Click **"Continue with Google"**.
- Pick the Google account that matches the email your organisation registered.
- You'll be signed in and redirected into the app.

:::note

If you see **"Your Google email isn't assigned to any organization or projects on Kaapi"**, your Google account doesn't match a registered user — contact [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org).

:::

#### Option 2 — Email me a login link (magic link)

- Enter your registered email and click **"Send login link"**.
- Check your inbox for the email from Kaapi.
- Click the link **within 15 minutes** (links expire after that).
- The link opens `/verify`, signs you in, and redirects you into the app.

### Step 4: Start using Kaapi

- Once signed in, the sidebar gives access to **Configurations**, **Datasets**, **Evaluations**, **Guardrails**, **Knowledge Base**, and more.
- Superusers additionally see **Settings** for organisation-level admin.

---

## Flow B — Connect with an X-API Key (Keystore)

Use this flow if your administrator has given you an API key instead of (or in addition to) a user invitation.

### Step 1: Obtain your API key

- Your Kaapi administrator generates and shares an API key with you out-of-band (email, secure password manager, etc.).

:::caution[Keep this key confidential]

Anyone with the key can act on its behalf.

:::

### Step 2: Open the Keystore

- Navigate to `/keystore` in the app.

:::note

Keystore is only accessible when **signed out**. If you're already signed in with a user account, sign out first (or simply use Flow A — you don't need a key).

:::

### Step 3: Add your API key

1. Click **"Add API Key"** to open the dialog.
2. Select **Provider = Kaapi**.
3. Give the key a **Label** (e.g., _"Production Key"_) so you can recognise it later.
4. Paste the API key into the **API Key** field.
5. Click **"Add API Key"**.

### Step 4: Validation and storage

- The key is validated against the backend (`POST /api/apikeys/verify`) before being saved.
- If validation fails, you'll see **"Invalid API key. Please check the key and try again."** — re-check the value with your administrator.
- Once valid, the key is stored in your browser's local storage and used to authorise API requests across the app.

### Step 5: Manage your key

- Only **one API key** can be stored at a time. To replace it, delete the existing key and add a new one.
- Use the action icons on the key card to:
  - 👁 **Show / hide** the key value
  - 📋 **Copy** the key to clipboard
  - 🗑 **Delete** the key

### Step 6: Start using Kaapi

- With a valid key in place, you can use **Evaluations** and related workflows that accept X-API key authentication.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| **"No account found"** on Google sign-in | Your Google email is not registered. Contact [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org). |
| **"Login link is invalid or has expired"** | Magic links expire after 15 minutes. Request a new one from the login screen. |
| **"Invitation link is invalid or has expired"** | Ask your administrator to re-send the invite. |
| **"Invalid API key"** in Keystore | Confirm the exact key value with your administrator; check for trailing spaces. |
| **Session expired while working** | Kaapi will sign you out automatically; sign back in to continue. |

:::tip[Need help?]

Reach out to [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org) or your organisation administrator.

:::
