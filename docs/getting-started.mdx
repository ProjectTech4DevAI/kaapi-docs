# Getting Started

As a Kaapi partner, your organisation will be onboarded by the Kaapi team or one of our delivery partners. The steps below help you get into the platform once that's set up.

---

## As a first-time user

### Step 1: Receive your invitation

- Your Kaapi administrator (or a delivery partner) invites your email address.
- You'll receive an invitation email from Kaapi.

### Step 2: Activate your account

- Open the invitation email and click the invite link.
- This activates your account and takes you to the sign-in screen.

:::caution[Invitation link issues]

If you see **"Invitation link is invalid or has expired"**, contact Kaapi support for a new link.

:::

### Step 3: Sign in

On the login screen, choose one of:

#### Option 1 — Continue with Google

- Click **"Continue with Google"**.
- Pick the Google account that matches the email your organisation registered.
- You'll be signed in and redirected into the app.

:::note

If you see **"Your Google email isn't assigned to any organization or projects on Kaapi"**, your Google account doesn't match a registered user — contact [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org).

:::

#### Option 2 — Email me a login link (magic link)

- Enter your registered email and click **"Send login link"**.
- Check your inbox for the email from Kaapi.
- Click the link **within 15 minutes** (links expire after that).
- You'll be signed in and redirected into the app.

### Step 4: Start using Kaapi

Once signed in, you'll see your organisation's workspace.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| **"No account found"** on Google sign-in | Your Google email is not registered. Contact [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org). |
| **"Login link is invalid or has expired"** | Magic links expire after 15 minutes. Request a new one from the login screen. |
| **"Invitation link is invalid or has expired"** | Ask your administrator to re-send the invite. |
| **Session expired while working** | Kaapi will sign you out automatically; sign back in to continue. |

:::tip[Need help?]

Reach out to [Kaapi Support](mailto:support-kaapi@projecttech4dev.org) or your organisation administrator.

:::
